### Slide 2: The Silent Retail Crisis
- **The Context:** Over 160 Million Demat accounts in India; rapid expansion into Tier-2, Tier-3, and rural Bharat.
- **The Attack Vector:** Unregistered Telegram/WhatsApp groups promising 200%–500% guaranteed jackpot returns, fake SEBI letterheads, cloned broker APKs, and personal UPI handles.
- **The Core Flaw of Post-Facto Dispute Resolution:** Once funds leave a savings account via UPI, the recovery rate is under 5%. Defense must be **pre-transaction**.

---

### Slide 3: Why Generic LLM Wrappers Fail
- **1. Regulatory Hallucinations:** When prompted with a fake registration number like `INH000999999`, general LLMs hallucinate validity simply because `INH` looks like a Research Analyst code.
- **2. The Regulatory Trapdoor:** Generic AI chatbots frequently violate SEBI rules by dispensing unauthorized stock tips or speculative market forecasts.
- **3. English-Only Legalistic Bias:** Tier-3 retail victims do not read 20-page SEBI PDF circulars in legal English.

- **SatarkBharat's Accurate Routing:**
