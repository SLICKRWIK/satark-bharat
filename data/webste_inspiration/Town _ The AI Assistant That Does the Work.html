Jess McKinney

Added the Park Slope kitchen remodel to Projects after Friday’s signed proposal from Francis Choi.

Noticed invoices go out at project milestones rather than monthly, and moved that into How she works. Learned she goes quiet on email during site days and catches up after six.

Overview

Jess McKinney runs a residential interior design studio in Brooklyn as a solo practitioner — client communication, sourcing, project management, and billing all sit with her. Four renovations are in construction and two more are out for proposal, tracked in Projects.

Her week is built around site days with contractor Jonathon D’Amore and clients like Alexis Swanson, which is why Tuesdays and Wednesdays rarely hold anything else. She bills at milestones rather than monthly, so cash flow follows project phases instead of the calendar.

How she works

Jess sends proposals on Monday mornings and follows up on Thursday if there has been no reply. Site visits are batched into Tuesdays and Wednesdays alongside Jonathon D’Amore, so email slows to a stop on those days and catches up in the evening.

Invoices go out at project milestones rather than on a monthly cycle — the piece of the week she would most like back, per Goals.

Goals

Take on two more renovations without hiring, which means proposal turnaround has to come down from about a week to two days.

Stop losing evenings to invoicing. The milestone billing described in How she works is the part of the job she would most like to hand off.

Projects

The West Village renovation for Alexis Swanson is in construction, with weekly walkthroughs alongside Jonathon D’Amore.

The Park Slope kitchen remodel for Francis Choi is waiting on tile selections from Dustin Leuschke this week. Two further proposals are out and awaiting client sign-off.

Professional identity

Jess McKinney runs a residential interior design studio in Brooklyn. As a solo practitioner she owns client communication, sourcing, project management, and billing, and is currently carrying four active renovations with two more in proposal.

Her email voice is warm, specific, and action-oriented — “Hi Alexis!” to open, “Thanks! Jess” to close, and rarely more than five sentences. The people she writes to most are on People.
